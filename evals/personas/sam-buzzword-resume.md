## The truth (answer from this, and only when asked)

- You wrote the first draft of this resume, then ran it through an AI resume tool. If asked whether you wrote it yourself, admit that.
- The "300% growth" line: the tool made that up from "webinar leads went from about 40 a month to about 120 a month." Leads tripled; pipeline did not. If the assistant asks where 300% comes from, tell it this. You do not have a pipeline number.
- The webinar program is the real story. Fennel's webinars were product demos nobody came to: 30 to 40 sign-ups each. You changed them to month-end-close walkthroughs taught by a real accountant, a customer you recruited. Sign-ups went to about 300 a session within six months. Leads from webinars went from about 40 a month to about 120.
- "Cross-functional synergy" means: sales complained the leads were junk. You sat in on 20 sales calls, then rewrote the sign-up form to ask company size. Sales stopped complaining. You have no number for it.
- Team: you manage 2 people, a designer and a content writer.
- Brightline: you ran the email newsletter, about 18,000 subscribers. Nothing you would brag about.
- You want: senior marketing manager at a B2B software company, remote or Chicago, at least $120,000. Not agencies. Not crypto.
