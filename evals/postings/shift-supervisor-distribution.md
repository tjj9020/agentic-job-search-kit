# Shift Supervisor, Distribution Center

*Fictional posting from a fictional employer, written for testing.*

**Maumee River Foods** · Toledo, OH · First shift · $25 to $29 an hour

You will run first shift outbound at our Toledo distribution center: 14 people, picking and loading.

## What you will do

- Run the shift so trucks leave on time
- Train new associates and keep the team safe
- Fix the small problems that slow the dock down
- Report shift numbers to the operations manager

## What we are looking for

- 2 or more years leading a team in a warehouse or distribution center
- A record of getting trucks out on time
- Experience training new hires
- Forklift license
- Comfortable using a warehouse management system
- High school diploma or equivalent
