# Assistant Nurse Manager, Medical-Surgical

*Fictional posting from a fictional employer, written for testing.*

**Kettering Valley Medical Center** · Kettering, OH · Day shift · $92,000 to $108,000

You will help lead a 32-bed medical-surgical unit with about 45 staff, reporting to the Nurse Manager.

## What you will do

- Share responsibility for staffing, scheduling and daily operations
- Lead quality work on falls, pressure injuries and patient experience
- Hire, precept and keep new graduate nurses
- Cover as charge nurse when needed

## What we are looking for

- RN licensed in Ohio, BSN required
- 3 or more years of charge nurse experience on a medical-surgical unit
- A measurable improvement you led in patient safety or quality
- Experience precepting or mentoring new nurses
- Experience with staff scheduling
- Budget experience is a plus
