# Code of conduct

People come to this project while looking for work, which is a hard time for many of them. Keep that in mind.

## Expected

- Be patient with people who are new to AI tools, GitHub, or both.
- Criticize ideas and wording, not people.
- Assume a confusing report is an honest one, and ask a question before closing it.
- Keep personal details out of issues and pull requests: yours and anyone else's.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind
- Posting someone's personal information
- Recruiting, advertising, or selling services in issues and pull requests

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block accounts that break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/tjj9020).
