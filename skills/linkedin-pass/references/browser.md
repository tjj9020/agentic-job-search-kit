# Using a browser on LinkedIn

You are acting inside the user's own signed-in account, in public, under their name. The limits below exist because a mistake here is visible to their employer and their network, and because LinkedIn can restrict accounts that look automated.

## How to work

- **They sign in.** If LinkedIn shows a sign-in page, a security check or a "verify it's you" step, stop and ask them to complete it. Never type or ask for a password or a code.
- **Only while they are present.** Not in a scheduled or unattended run.
- **One change at a time.** Show before and after, wait for a yes, make the edit, then read the page back to confirm it saved as agreed.
- **Turn off network sharing** before saving, when LinkedIn offers to share the edit.
- **Profile edits only.** No posts, comments, reactions, connection requests, messages, follows or settings.
- **Before removing anything LinkedIn says can't be undone** (a skill with endorsements, a job, a recommendation), say exactly what will be lost and get a second yes.

## When it doesn't work

Say what happened in plain words and keep going. Two tries at most, and never go quiet.

- **Not signed in:** ask them to sign in, in the browser window, and wait.
- **A field won't take the text, or the page isn't what you expected:** hand them that piece to paste in themselves and carry on with the next.
- **The browser stops responding:** say you have lost it and give them the rest as text to paste.

They should finish the sitting knowing which changes are on LinkedIn and which are still theirs to make. Record both in the log.
