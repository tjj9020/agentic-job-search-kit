# Where the job report looks

This is the complete list of places the job report searches out of the box. Nothing is searched that is not on this list, on the user's own list, or named by the user in feedback.

These defaults are for the United States. For a user in another country, replace "Everyone" and "Government" with that country's main job boards and public job service, tell the user which ones you chose, and save them under "Where to look" in their search criteria.

## Everyone

| Source | What it is | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Indeed | General job board | Often visible in search results but not openable. If so, find the same job on the employer's site. |
| LinkedIn Jobs | General job board | Same limit as Indeed. Never sign in or use the user's account. |
| Glassdoor | General job board, with pay and reviews | Also used for pay ranges and culture evidence |
| ZipRecruiter | General job board | |

## Employers' own hiring pages

Most employers post on a hiring system before a job reaches the boards, and these pages are the most reliable proof a job is still open. Search for the job title together with each system's name.

| Hiring system | Its pages look like | Used most by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greenhouse | `boards.greenhouse.io`, `job-boards.greenhouse.io` | Software and startups |
| Lever | `jobs.lever.co` | Software and startups |
| Ashby | `jobs.ashbyhq.com` | Startups |
| Workday | `myworkdayjobs.com` | Large companies, hospitals, universities |
| iCIMS | `icims.com` | Large companies, healthcare, retail |
| SmartRecruiters | `jobs.smartrecruiters.com` | Retail, logistics, hospitality |
| Workable | `apply.workable.com` | Small and mid-size companies |
| ADP | `workforcenow.adp.com` | Small and mid-size employers, trades |

## Government

| Source | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| USAJOBS | Federal jobs |
| GovernmentJobs | State, county and city jobs |
| CareerOneStop | The public job bank for each state, run with the Department of Labor |

Search these only when the user's criteria allow public-sector work.

## By kind of work

Pick the rows that match the job the user wants. Use at most two.

| Kind of work | Also search |
| --- | --- |
| Software, product, data, design | Built In, Wellfound, Y Combinator's Work at a Startup |
| Nursing and other healthcare | Health eCareers, and the careers pages of hospital systems within the user's travel distance |
| Warehouse, driving, retail, hourly work | Snagajob, and the careers pages of the largest employers within the user's travel distance |
| Schools and universities | HigherEdJobs, and the careers pages of school districts and colleges nearby |
| Nonprofit | Idealist |
| Remote work, any field | We Work Remotely |
| Anything else | Ask the user once: "Where do people in your line of work usually find jobs?" and use the answer |

## Local

One search for the job title with the user's city or region, to catch local employers and regional boards the lists above miss.

## The user's own list

- **Employers they name.** If the search criteria list places the user would like to work, check each one's careers page directly on every run.
- **Sources they add or remove.** "Also check X" and "stop checking Y" are saved under "Where to look" in the search criteria and obeyed from then on.

## A connected job board

If the app has a direct connection to a job board, use it before web search for that board. It is more reliable than reading web pages. Say in the report that you used it.

## How the ten searches are spent

| Searches | On |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 3 | The job title and location on the general boards |
| 4 to 6 | The job title on employers' hiring pages |
| 7 to 8 | The two "by kind of work" sources |
| 9 | Local |
| 10 | Government if allowed, otherwise the user's named employers or a second job title |

Named employers beyond what fits in the ten are checked in rotation, a few each run.

## What is not searched

- Anything that needs the user to sign in
- Staffing-agency listings with no named employer
- Social media posts, forums and message boards
- Paid job boards
