Added `job-search`, a single entry point. Run `/job-search` and it reads your documents, shows a short status board, recommends one next step and takes you there, so you never have to pick between the other skills. `career-setup` no longer opens with a full resume audit: it names the three weakest spots, saves the rest as open questions for the story bank, and imports a story bank or voice profile you already have.
