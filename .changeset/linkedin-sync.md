LinkedIn is now kept in step with your resume, not fixed once and forgotten. The kit remembers what your profile should say, shows a LinkedIn line on your status board, reminds you of changes you approved but haven't pasted in, and tells you what to update when your stories, dates or target jobs change. It still never posts, connects or messages for you, and it won't add a public job-hunting line unless you ask.
