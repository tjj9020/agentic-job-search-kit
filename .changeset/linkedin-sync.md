LinkedIn is now kept in step with your resume, not fixed once and forgotten. The kit remembers what your profile should say, shows a LinkedIn line on your status board, reminds you of changes you approved but haven't pasted in, and tells you what to update when your stories, dates or target jobs change. In a desktop app with a browser, it can make the edits for you while you watch, one approved change at a time; in a terminal it says it can't and gives you the text to paste. It still never posts, connects or messages for you, and it won't add a public job-hunting line unless you ask.
