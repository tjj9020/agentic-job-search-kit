## The resume

**JORDAN RIVERA**
Columbus, OH · jordan.rivera@example.com

**SUMMARY**

I run fulfillment centers and fix the things that make them ship late or ship wrong. For the last four years that has been a 140-person, two-shift building at Northfield Freight, where mis-ships fell from 2.1% to 0.6% and late peak shipments fell from 11% to 3%.

**EXPERIENCE**

**Operations Manager, Northfield Freight**, Columbus, OH · 2021 to present

Run a 140-person fulfillment center on two shifts through 2 shift managers and 9 leads, with a $9.4M labor budget.

- 2.1% of orders were shipping wrong and our largest customer had put the account on a 90-day review. Most errors happened at pack, so I added a scan check at each pack station instead of hiring more inspectors. Order accuracy went from 97.9% to 99.4% in nine months and the review ended.
- In the 2023 peak we shipped 11% of orders late on six weeks of mandatory overtime. Moved the top 200 items to the front of the building and cross-trained 30 associates so labor could follow the bottleneck. 2024 peak: 3% late, 22% less overtime, 9% more volume.
- The vendor's plan for our warehouse management system migration called for a five-day shutdown. Ran three full rehearsals on a copy of live inventory and cut over in one weekend. No missed ship days.
- Nearly half of new hires were leaving within 90 days. Fixed their schedules for the first three months and paired each with a trained buddy. 90-day attrition went from 48% to 27% in a year.

**Shift Supervisor, Brightway Parcel**, Dayton, OH · 2017 to 2021

- Started as an associate and was promoted twice in four years. Ran second shift, 35 people, and trained its new hires.

**SKILLS**

Order accuracy and quality, peak season planning, warehouse management system migration, labor planning and budgeting, slotting, onboarding and retention
