# Director of Fulfillment Operations

*Fictional posting from a fictional company, written for this example.*

**Larkspur Home Goods** · Columbus, OH (hybrid, 4 days on site) · $135,000 to $160,000 base, 15% bonus target

## About the role

You will run our two fulfillment centers, in Columbus and Indianapolis, and the 250 people who work in them. Our Indianapolis building is 14 months old and its order accuracy is not where Columbus is. Fixing that is your first job. Your second is a peak plan that treats the two buildings as one network.

You report to the VP of Supply Chain and have two site managers reporting to you.

## What you will do

- Own safety, quality, on-time shipping and cost per order across both sites
- Bring Indianapolis order accuracy in line with Columbus
- Build and run the peak season plan across the network
- Lead the floor side of the warehouse management system rollout at Indianapolis
- Own an operating budget of about $18M
- Hire, develop and keep site leaders

## What we are looking for

- 7 or more years in fulfillment or distribution operations, with at least 3 leading a building
- Experience leading more than one site, or leading managers of managers
- A record of measurable gains in order accuracy and on-time shipping
- Experience planning and running a peak season
- Hands-on experience with a warehouse management system migration
- Budget or P&L ownership
- Lean or continuous improvement training is a plus
