## 1. Director of Fulfillment Operations, Larkspur Home Goods: 82/100

[Posting](https://example.com/larkspur/careers/1042) · Columbus, OH, hybrid (4 days on site) · posted March 4

**The business.** Larkspur sells home goods online and through about 40 stores in the Midwest. It ships from two fulfillment centers, in Columbus and Indianapolis.

**Growth.** Online orders grew 31% last year according to the company's annual letter. The Indianapolis building opened 14 months ago.

**The role.** Runs both fulfillment centers, about 250 people, through two site managers. Reports to the VP of Supply Chain. The posting names two problems: order accuracy at the new building and a peak plan that covers both sites.

**Why you fit**
- They want someone to fix order accuracy at a newer site. You cut mis-ships from 2.1% to 0.6% in nine months by moving the check to the pack station.
- They want a peak plan. You took late peak shipments from 11% to 3% on 9% more volume and 22% less overtime.
- They list a WMS rollout at Indianapolis this year. You cut over a WMS in one weekend with no missed ship days.

**Gaps or risks**
- You have run one site, not two, and you have not managed site managers.
- They ask for P&L ownership of about $18M. You own a $9.4M labor budget, not a full P&L.

**Culture.** Two of the three site leaders listed on the company's careers page started as hourly associates. Safety is the first metric in the annual letter's operations section. Evidence: moderate, all from the company's own pages.

**Pay and benefits.** Base $135K to $160K (posted). Bonus target 15% (posted). Benefits not detailed in the posting.
