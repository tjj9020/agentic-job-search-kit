# Interview brief: Larkspur Home Goods, hiring manager round

*Fictional example of the one-page brief `interview-prep` delivers.*

**Role:** Director of Fulfillment Operations · **Stage:** hiring manager (VP of Supply Chain), 45 minutes

## The three stories that matter

| If they ask about | Tell | The number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixing order accuracy | Mis-ships and the pack-station scan | 2.1% to 0.6% in nine months |
| Peak across the network | Peak season: late shipments | 11% late to 3%, 22% less overtime, 9% more volume |
| The Indianapolis WMS rollout | WMS cutover | One weekend, zero missed ship days, three rehearsals |

Backup: first-90-day attrition, 48% to 27%. Use it for any question about people, but only once.

## Likely questions

1. Indianapolis accuracy is behind Columbus. How would you approach it? → Mis-ships story. Start with how you found where the errors happened, not the fix.
2. Tell me about a peak that went badly. → 2023 first, then 2024. Do not skip 2023.
3. How do you run a system cutover without stopping the floor? → WMS cutover.
4. You have run one building. Why are you ready for two? → Gap, see below.
5. Have you owned a P&L? → Gap, see below.
6. How do you develop the managers under you? → Weak. You have 2 shift managers and no story in the bank yet. Decide before the interview which one you will talk about.
7. Why are you leaving Northfield? → Not in your documents. Tell me and I will help you say it in two sentences.

## Gaps, and what to say

**One site, not two.** "I've run one building. What I've done there is what your Indianapolis site needs first: find where the errors happen and fix it at that station. What I'd be learning is doing that through a site manager instead of on the floor myself, and I'd want to spend my first month in Indianapolis."

**Labor budget, not a P&L.** "I own a $9.4M labor budget. I haven't owned the full P&L. I'd want to sit down with finance in week one and learn the lines I haven't managed."

## Questions to ask them

1. What is Indianapolis's order accuracy today, and where do the errors happen?
2. Do the two buildings share a peak plan now, or run separate ones?
3. How will you know in six months that this hire was the right one?
4. Where did the two site managers come from, and how long have they been in the role?
5. What does the WMS rollout timeline look like against peak?

## Open item

Your story bank still has no confirmed inventory accuracy figure for the WMS cutover. If you can get it from the cycle count report before the interview, use it. If not, say "no missed ship days" and stop there.
