# Resume Voice Profile: Jordan Rivera

Status: v1.0, approved by Jordan March 5. *Fictional example.*

## Sources analyzed
- Peak season recap email to the regional VP (2024)
- Proposal for pack-station scanning (2022)
- Two shift-lead performance reviews Jordan wrote
- EXCLUDED: the current resume summary. Jordan wrote it with an AI tool. Its facts are usable; its wording is not a voice source.

## Observed rules
1. Starts with what was going wrong, in floor terms. "We were inspecting mistakes after we'd already taped them shut."
2. Short sentences. Rarely more than one clause.
3. Numbers come as before and after, with the time it took. "11% late last year. 3% this year."
4. Names the place the work happens: pack station, dock, second shift, the back of the building. Never "the operation."
5. Says what was turned down. "I didn't need more checkers."
6. Admits the cost. The scanning proposal says pack rate will drop at first, and by how much.
7. Says "we" for the work and "I" for the decision.
8. No adjectives about self. No "strong," "proven," "passionate."
9. Dry humor, once, never twice in a document.

## Words
Preferred: fixed, cut, moved, the floor, shift, late, wrong, on time
Banned (default list): spearheaded, leveraged, orchestrated, championed, synergy, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, passionate, results-driven, proven track record, transformative, innovative, fast-paced environment
Jordan's additions: "optimize," "stakeholders," "best-in-class," "team player." Test: would Jordan say it at the 2 p.m. shift meeting?

## Style picks
- Verbs: plain ("cut," "moved") over elevated ("drove," "transformed")
- Order: situation first, then the move, then the result
- Edge: dry and factual
- Summary: opens with the kind of problem Jordan fixes, not the title

## Gold examples (approved by Jordan)
- 2.1% of orders were shipping wrong and our biggest customer had us on a 90-day review. Most errors happened at pack, so I put a scan check there instead of hiring more inspectors. Nine months later the rate was 0.6% and the review was over.
- In 2023 we shipped 11% of peak orders late on six weeks of mandatory overtime. Moved the top 200 items to the front and cross-trained 30 people. 2024: 3% late, 22% less overtime, 9% more volume.
- Nearly half of new hires were gone in 90 days. Fixed their schedules and gave each one a buddy. Attrition went from 48% to 27%.
- Summary model: "I run fulfillment centers and fix the things that make them ship late or ship wrong."

## Notes on the current base resume
- The summary could describe anyone. "Results-driven" and "proven track record" are on the banned list.
- Six bullets, no numbers. "Improved order accuracy through process improvements" hides the best story in the building.
- Every bullet is one verb plus one object. That uniform shape is the main AI-template tell.
