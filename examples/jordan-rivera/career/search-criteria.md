# Search Criteria: Jordan Rivera

Approved by Jordan, March 3. *Fictional example.*

## Candidate profile

Operations Manager at Northfield Freight in Columbus, OH since 2021, running a 140-person, two-shift fulfillment center with a $9.4M labor budget. Cut the mis-ship rate from 2.1% to 0.6% in nine months by adding scan verification at pack stations. Cut late shipments during peak from 11% of orders to 3% year over year while overtime hours fell 22%. Cut first-90-day attrition from 48% to 27%. Led the floor cutover to a new warehouse management system over one weekend with no missed ship days. Before that, four years at Brightway Parcel, promoted from Associate to Shift Supervisor of 35 people. A.S. in Business Management. Has run one site; has not run multiple sites or owned a full P&L.

## Hard filters

1. Location: on-site or hybrid within 40 minutes of Columbus, OH, or fully remote
2. Level: Senior Operations Manager, Director of Operations, General Manager, or equivalent
3. Pay: base of $125K or more when the posting states it
4. Posted within the last 30 days and still open on the employer's own site

## Scoring preferences

Default weights. Within "role and scope fit", favor roles with more than one site or a full P&L, since that is the growth Jordan wants.

## Culture: what must be true

- Safety numbers are reported to leadership as often as output numbers
- Managers are promoted from the floor, with examples
- Performance data is visible to the people doing the work

## Deal-breakers

- Third-party logistics roles with more than 50% travel
- Permanent overnight shift
- Staffing-agency postings with no named employer
